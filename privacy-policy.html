Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Soma Insurance Group (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Business and vehicle information you provide when requesting an insurance quote, such as business name, DOT/MC number, vehicle details, and coverage needs.
  • Records of our communications with you, including text messages and calls.

How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare and deliver insurance quotes you requested.
  • To contact you about quotes, coverage options, appointments, and follow-up related to your request.
  • To provide certificates of insurance, filings, and policy service.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

SMS Text Messaging

When you request a quote or information from Soma Insurance Group and provide your mobile number, you consent to receive text messages from us about your request. Message frequency varies. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed to obtain quotes and service policies—for example, with insurance carriers and underwriting partners for the purpose of quoting and binding coverage you requested—or as required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information.
